Ptolemaic Astronomy & Astrology — Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

This app collects no personal data. No account is required to use it.

2. Location

If you tap the 📍 GPS button, your device location is used locally to position the observer on the map. It is never transmitted to or stored on any server.

2a. Camera (AR Sky Overlay)

If you open the 👁 AR Sky Overlay, the app requests access to the rear camera and the device's orientation sensors. The camera feed is composited locally with the catalog overlay and discarded each frame — no still images, no video, no orientation history is recorded or transmitted. Camera and orientation access stop the moment you close the overlay.

3. Local Storage

The app saves your settings (map projection, observer position, playback speed, theme preference) in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

4. Third Parties

No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party services of any kind are included.

5. Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/stpierrs/conceptual_flat_earth_model .
