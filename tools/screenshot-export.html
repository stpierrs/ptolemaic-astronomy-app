Screenshot exporter

Each preview renders the app at exact target pixel dimensions via iframe size = target/dpr + transform: scale(dpr). The Capture button uses html2canvas at scale: 1 so the saved PNG is the literal pixel size required by the store listing.
Required dimensions reference
  • Google Play phone: min 1080×1920, max 3840×3840 — 2 to 8 shots.
  • Google Play 7" tablet: min 1080×1920.
  • Google Play 10" tablet: min 1080×1920.
  • Google Play feature graphic: 1024×500 (use the "Feature graphic" preset).
  • Apple 6.9" Display phone: 1290×2796 (iPhone 15/16 Pro Max).
  • Apple 6.5" Display phone: 1284×2778 or 1242×2688 (XS Max / 11 Pro Max).
  • Apple 6.1" Display phone: 1170×2532 (iPhone 14/15 non-Pro).
  • Apple 13" iPad Pro: 2064×2752 (M4).
  • Apple 12.9" iPad Pro: 2048×2732 (6th gen).
